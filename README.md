### 🛠 What I Build

I specialize in networking, distributed systems, and infrastructure problems. In particular, I've done a lot of work with hybrid cloud/on-prem setups, APIs for airgapped environments, distributed infrastructure across regions.

I just wrapped up a summer at **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure** (OCI) in Redwood Shores. Before that, at **Viasat**, I built the control and management web tool and REST API for their Next Generation Modem in three weeks, helping secure a **$250K product order**, and represented the team at **IEEE MILCOM 2025**. Previously: GN&C systems engineering on Trident II at **Draper Laboratory**, neutron scattering simulations at **NIST**. As Director of Development at ACM UCLA, I led a 25-person engineering team establishing production infrastructure for 1,000+ members - open-source, community-owned, and free. This Fall: **SpaceX Starlink infrastructure**.

### 🔓 Open Source & Projects

Most of what I know came from leveraging open source tools (notably Proxmox and Rathole), and giving back is the least I can do. I created and maintain **[gopher](https://github.com/smalex-z/gopher)** (a FOSS self-hosted Cloudflare Tunnel alternative), **[nimbus](https://github.com/smalex-z/nimbus)** (FOSS AWS/OpenStack Alternative), and **[STIMViewer](https://github.com/smalex-z/STIMViewer)** (a GPU-accelerated image processing pipeline for fluorescence neural activity analysis). I've also contributed to **[SasView](https://github.com/SasView/sasview)**, a scientific neutron scattering analysis tool used by researchers worldwide.
