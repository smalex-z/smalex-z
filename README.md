### 🛠 What I Build

I specialize in networking, distributed systems, and the kind of infrastructure problems where downtime isn't an option. My work spans the full stack from bare-metal Proxmox clusters to web APIs, and I'm most energized by problems that force you to think about the whole system: networking, virtualization, databases, deployment.

At **Viasat**, I built the control interface for our virtualized satellite modem in three weeks, helped secure our first **$250K product order**, and represented the company at **IEEE MILCOM 2025**. Before that, I did MBSE/SysML work on Trident II guidance and navigation systems at **Charles Stark Draper Laboratory**, and ran neutron scattering simulations at **NIST**. This summer, I'm heading to **Oracle OCI** as a SWE Intern.

### 🌐 Infrastructure & Self-Hosting

I'm the sysadmin and creator of **three cross-country server clusters** hosting personal and production workloads for UCLA's largest CS organization, cutting costs by thousands while learning what it actually takes to keep systems reliable at scale. My stack of choice: Proxmox + Docker Compose + Caddy, with rathole for NAT traversal and reverse tunneling.

For **[ACM@UCLA](https://uclaacm.com)**, I designed and shipped the org's entire self-hosted infrastructure on a 5-node Proxmox cluster in Boelter Hall, including an Outline wiki, Uptime Kuma status monitoring, Immich photo management, and a custom rathole tunnel gateway through an OCI ARM instance. Whether it's architecting hybrid setups that leverage both cloud and on-prem, building APIs that work in airgapped environments, or managing distributed infrastructure across regions: if it can be self-hosted, it probably should be.
